# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

The first version.

- Telling a dream on waking: a dark, dim screen, speaking or typing, and fading words. A morning note and a lock-screen widget open it.
- Each dream becomes a comic page in the spirit of Little Nemo. The app makes first-guess panels from the words, built from 31 places, 28 actions, 8 moods and a cast. Each mood has its own frame shape, the captions are your own words, and every page ends with you waking in bed.
- An editor for the panels: where, who, what, how it felt, words, and a finger drawing that the app inks.
- An atlas of named places, with doors between them, fog, and Nemo's walking bed.
- A cast built once from a few choices. Nameless figures who come back can join it.
- New endings for bad dreams. The original stays on the back of the page, and an evening reminder asks you to read the new ending.
- Lucid-dream stars.
- English and Russian.
