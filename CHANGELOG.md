# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

The first version, a lucid-dreaming trainer.

- **Morning:** tell a dream on a dark, dim screen, typing or speaking, with fading words. Then mark how clear it was: no, a glimmer, or lucid.
- **Dream signs:** found in the journal by themselves, from 21 common signs in Russian and English, plus signs of your own.
- **Day:** reality checks at moments you can't guess, with "Checked" right in the notification.
- **Night:** the MILD ritual. Recall a dream, look at tonight's sign, then hold the top while repeating the intention.
- **The top:** practice winds it up. Lucid dreams add brass rings, and the morning after one it doesn't fall.
- English and Russian. No account, no internet.
